Every ride inspects the cycleway.

Smart Cycle is an AI-powered app that detects bumps, cracks and potholes on Dublin’s cycleways while people ride, and turns them into a map cities can act on.

Partner with usGet the app

Cycleways are inspected rarely, and it costs a lot

8% → 12% of Dublin adults cycle five or more days a week, between 2023 and 2025

More people ride every year, yet cycleways are rarely inspected. Manual inspection is expensive, and cycling budgets are being cut. Rough surfaces hit safety, accessibility and comfort.

How it works

  1. Ride

    Open the app and cycle as usual.

  2. Detect

    The phone senses irregularities and the classifier labels them.

  3. Locate

    GPS and map-matching place each one on the right cycleway.

  4. Add proof

    After the ride, share a photo to support your report.

Try the demo

What each group gets

Partner cities

Full analysis, a heatmap of road quality and alerts for the worst sections, so maintenance goes where it is needed.

Riders

Safer cycleways, and a simple way to back up a report with a photo.

The public

All reported data stays viewable online, without the analysis.

Why it matters for the city

  • No inspection vehicles needed
  • Real-time data
  • Targeted maintenance
  • Lower logistics costs
  • Fewer accidents
  • More citizen participation
